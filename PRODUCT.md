# KE/Y — Kenichi Edbert Yauwanta Portfolio

## Register
brand

## Users
Growth Lead and Founding Growth hiring managers at Seed-to-Series-B startups. They open 20 portfolios a day. They decide in 15 seconds. They are pattern-matching for someone who has both driven growth AND understands how to build the systems that support it.

## Brand Voice
Direct. Human. Not humble-braggy. Not corporate. The work speaks first, the person speaks second. One surprising detail is worth ten bullet points. Confidence without ego.

## Core Narrative
Two acts. Act 1: the community marketer — someone who builds real human communities that produce real results (110K downloads, 15K Discord, Rp 120M/month). Act 2: the reveal — underneath that is someone who builds the technical systems those communities run on. Solo. In production. The combination is rare.

## Tagline
"Marketing by trade. Systems by obsession."

## Anti-References
- LinkedIn profile rendered as a website (credentials in sections, no story)
- SaaS metrics dashboard (four equal stat boxes, no hierarchy)
- Generic tech portfolio (dark + amber + terminal = 10,000 other sites)
- Any site where you could replace the name and it would still work

## Strategic Principles
- Photos earn their space by telling the story, not decorating it
- Every number lives inside the context that makes it meaningful
- The aesthetic shift between Act 1 and Act 2 is the point — it should feel like a reveal
- Less is more: fewer sections, fewer words, more weight on what remains
- The site should feel like it was made by someone with taste, not assembled from parts
