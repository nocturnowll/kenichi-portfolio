## The Problem

IchiEcha needed to launch a new F&B product — Dubai Chewy Cookies — in Samarinda's local market, with no existing brand equity and a kitchen operation starting from zero.

## What I Did

Supported the full launch: channel strategy, GrabFood onboarding, pricing architecture, and the operational tracking layer that makes the business legible day-to-day.

**Channels:** Walk-in (home store), online (direct transfer orders via social), and GrabFood. Each channel has different economics — I mapped the margins and set pricing accordingly.

**ichiecha-ops:** I built a real-time sales and production tracking system that logs every transaction, tracks ingredient consumption, and reports daily and weekly revenue by channel. It's the data layer the business runs on.

## The Outcome

- Multi-channel distribution live across 3 channels
- DC-001 Pistachio Kunafa — bestselling SKU with 77% gross margin
- Real-time sales and COGS tracking via ichiecha-ops
- GrabFood listing live with consistent order volume
