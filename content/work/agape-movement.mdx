## The Problem

A new Pilates franchise in Samarinda — no existing members, no brand awareness, a studio that needed to be set up from scratch, and revenue targets that started day one.

## What I Did

I ran the full pre-launch and growth operation solo.

**Pre-launch:** Designed and ran a founders membership campaign that generated Rp 50M+ in revenue before the doors opened. That gave us a committed base and cash flow before the first class ran.

**Week one:** Filled 10-12 classes at capacity through hyper-local GTM — community outreach, micro-influencer activations, Meta Ads, and a trial-week offer that spread by word of mouth.

**Ongoing:** I'm the entire marketing and ops function. Meta Ads, graphic design, customer relations, monthly financial reporting. No team, no agency.

**The build layer:** Rather than stitching together off-the-shelf tools that didn't fit, I built what we needed — an AI-powered CRM for member communication (Claude-backed, WhatsApp and Instagram), a booking system replacing a commercial tool, and a financial reporting dashboard. All built solo using Claude Code.

## The Outcome

- **Rp 50M+** generated by pre-launch campaign before opening day
- **Rp 300M+ (~$20,000 USD)** in revenue within 90 days
- **Rp 120M/month** sustained omset — unlocking the 30% royalty tier
- **384 registered members**
- Sold-out classes in opening week
