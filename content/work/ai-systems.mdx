## The Problem

Every business I operate had the same gap: the tools that existed were built for someone else's workflow. Off-the-shelf CRMs don't speak Bahasa Indonesia or understand Pilates studio membership tiers. Generic booking software doesn't connect to the financial intelligence a franchise operator actually needs.

So I built them.

## What I Built

**crm-agent** — AI-powered CRM and messaging inbox for Agape Movement. Handles WhatsApp and Instagram conversations, uses Claude as an identity-concealed AI engine for member follow-up, connects to the booking system for context, and routes low-confidence messages to human review.

**booking-agent** — Full booking and payment system for Pilates classes. Client portal, admin reconciliation, sales adjustment tools, financial reporting. Replacing a commercial third-party tool.

**fnb-agent** — Multi-tenant F&B POS system with owner dashboard, QR ordering, loyalty tracking, and real-time inventory. Deployed at IchiEcha.

**ichiecha-ops** — Sales and production tracking for IchiEcha. Tracks every transaction, ingredient usage, and revenue split across channels.

**job-hunter** — Automated job discovery system. Scrapes three job boards every 8 hours, filters by role and location, writes matching jobs to a vault as markdown files, and sends a Telegram digest. Built during this job search.

## The AI Angle

Every system here was built solo using Claude Code as the primary development environment. The process: spec in conversation, generate scaffold, review and refine, ship. What would take a small team 2-3 months takes 1-2 weeks.

The job-hunter was built *during* this job search — it's finding the roles I'm applying to while you read this.

## The Outcome

- 5 production systems live and running
- All built solo using AI-assisted development
- Zero external contractors
- Total build time across all 5: approximately 8 weeks
